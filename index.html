Bake Rush Factory 3D

A cosy bakery tycoon for iPhone and iPad

Bake bread, cakes, croissants, donuts, cookies and cupcakes. Serve hungry customers, load delivery trucks, unlock new machines and grow a tiny bakery into a busy factory.

What you can do

Bake & collect

Run bread, cake, croissant, donut, cookie and cupcake machines, then carry fresh stacks around the factory.

Serve customers

Keep the service counters stocked, open a second counter and a vending plaza as your bakery grows.

Ship by truck

Fill delivery orders on the conveyor and send trucks out for big payouts.

Hire a crew

Bring in workers and cleaning robots to keep the factory spotless and running.

Grab power-ups

Glowing floor power-ups give coin bonuses, speed, extra carry space and faster production.

Missions every day

Complete missions and daily missions for cash rewards and keep levelling up.
