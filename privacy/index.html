Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy explains how Bake Rush Factory 3D (the "App"), published by Kinexapps ("we", "us"), handles information. We designed the App to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, no sign-in and we do not run our own servers for the App.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level, in-game cash, unlocked areas, upgrades, missions, settings such as music, sound and vibration, and whether you own Premium) is saved only on your device. We cannot see or access it. Deleting the App deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's IP address (used to estimate general location), device and operating-system information, app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data, and advertising identifiers where you allow them.

  • Tracking: the App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) is not made available to advertisers. Ads may still be selected using contextual and other non-tracking information.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent choices before ads are personalised. You can change your choices at any time in Settings > Ad Privacy Choices.
  • US state privacy laws: where applicable, the same Ad Privacy Choices option lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising.

Learn more about how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Rewarded ads

Some rewards (for example floor power-ups) are optional and are granted after you choose to watch a rewarded ad. The same AdMob data practices apply.

4. In-app purchases

Premium is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your name, payment card or billing details. The App receives only a confirmation from Apple that the purchase or restore was successful.

5. Information we receive when you contact us

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

6. Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above (Google for advertising, Apple for purchases) so that the App can work.

8. Data retention and your rights

Game data stays on your device until you delete the App. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or restrict its use. Contact us and we will help, including by pointing you to Google's tools for advertising data.

9. Security

We rely on Apple's and Google's security measures for purchases and advertising, and we keep any support emails private.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
