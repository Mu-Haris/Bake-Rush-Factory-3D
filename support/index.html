Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the game, tap the Settings (gear) button and tap Restore Purchases. You can also tap Restore Purchases in the Go Premium window. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium.

What does Premium include?

Premium is a one-time purchase that removes forced advertisements (banner, interstitial and app-open ads). Optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-game bonuses stay available.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Why did a power-up or rewarded ad not give me a reward?

Rewards are granted only after a rewarded ad has been watched to the end. If the ad was closed early or could not load, no reward is given; step on the power-up again to retry. If you watched a full ad and did not receive the reward, email us with the time it happened.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new device?

Progress is saved automatically on your device. The game has no accounts, so progress is not synced between devices and deleting the app removes it. Premium can always be restored on a new device with Restore Purchases.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Open Settings and tap Ad Privacy Choices (shown where required by local law). See our Privacy Policy for details.

The game runs slowly or crashes

Close other apps, restart your device and make sure iOS and the game are up to date. If it keeps happening, email us your device model and iOS version.
